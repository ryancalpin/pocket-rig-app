# Pocket Rig Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05

## The short version

- Pocket Rig collects no data about you.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
- It talks only to your own gaming PC.
- The one outside contact is Steam's public image servers, for cover art (details below).

## What stays on your devices

- **Your PC's address and name:** saved on your iPhone so you can reconnect.
- **Your pairing certificate:** a secret key made on your iPhone when you pair. It lives in the iOS Keychain, Apple's secure storage.
- **Your settings, buttons, and layouts:** saved on your iPhone.
- **Widget info:** a small amount of data (like your PC's name and status) is shared between the app and its widget through an App Group, a private storage area on your iPhone.
- None of this is sent to the developer.

## What Pocket Rig sends over the network

- **To your PC:** pairing, the game stream, your controller input, and settings. This goes to your PC (Sunshine, and the optional Helper app), not to the developer.
- **Finding your PC:** the app looks for your PC on your home network. iOS asks your permission for this ("Local Network").
- **Waking your PC:** the app can send a standard Wake-on-LAN message to your home network.
- **Steam cover art (optional Helper only):** if you use the optional Helper app, game cover art loads from Steam's public image servers (cdn.cloudflare.steamstatic.com). Valve, which runs those servers, can see your IP address and which art was requested. The developer never sees this, and no identifier is attached.

## Photos

- Pocket Rig can save screenshots and replay clips to your photo library.
- It has add-only access. It cannot read your photos.
- Nothing is uploaded.

## Tips

- The optional tip jar uses Apple's in-app purchase system.
- Apple handles the payment. The developer does not receive your payment details or identity.

## Tracking and third parties

- No tracking, and no advertising.
- No analytics or crash-reporting tools.
- No third-party services that receive your data.

## Children

- Pocket Rig collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your control

- Delete the app to remove everything it saved on your iPhone.
- You can turn off Local Network or Photos access any time in iOS Settings.

## Changes

- If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
